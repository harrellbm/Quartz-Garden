> [!Summary] The Big Idea
> Coram Mundo translated literally means "before Man" and refers to our relationship with other humans and the creation at large. It is in this relationship that we are called to actively do things and is where we express active (or proper) righteousness. "God does not need our good works but our neighbor does" as the saying goes.
