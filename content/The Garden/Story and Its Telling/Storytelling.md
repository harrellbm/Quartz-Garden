My attitude toward story as I grew

Story as walking in a different world

Taught that story was memory

Story as unnecessary extra

Story as entertainment

Story as wisdom

Story as a wild way to tell the truth

Story as bridge between the wild and the domesticated.

# Interesting Articles on Storytelling

[Gordon Brander on Method](https://gordonbrander.com/pattern/storytelling/)

I have really started to like what Martin Shaw has to say about storytelling. I find myself realizing that the kind of stories he tells are the ones I have been looking for but didn't know how to find.

Especially his assertion that telling stories about Jesus should be like they happened this morning. Because Jesus is about and in the present just as much as he was in those stories.

Here are some of his videos I have found helpful:

- This is his youtube page: [Jawbone with Martin Shaw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aCAyvu9wyEo) Goes through and does a lot of story telling as well as talking about how it can be used well.
- [the making of a storyteller](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LkREhU7-Gqc) Talks about his opinion about how one becomes a storyteller
- [Q and A Session](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TrkAr4TiVd0\&pp=QAFIAQ%3D%3D) Telling Jesus stories like they happened this morning or to you
- [SO, YOU WANT TO BE A STORYTELLER? | The Sense and Matter of Apprenticeship](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KI9jt6NQw9w) Talks through the practicalities of leaning how to tell stories
