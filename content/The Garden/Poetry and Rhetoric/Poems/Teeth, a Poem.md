\#🌿Sapling   #✒️Poem  #🔥Doctrine-Poetry-Story

---

<center>A story without teeth won't do anything much.</center>
<center>Just like a knife without an edge,</center>
<center>Or a nail without a point.</center>

<center>But a story with teeth bites deep into the heart.</center>
<center>Yet though the teeth be sharp and the edge cut deep, </center>
<center>If the wounds they reveal are beautiful and real,</center>
<center>Then there you'll find something worth shouting loud and clear.</center>

<center>An innocent man goes willingly to die. </center>
<center>The only pure man bitten by a snake,</center>
<center>With iron teeth piercing hands and feet. </center>
<center>But the blood of the man drowns the snake,</center>
<center>And out of its loose coils slowly stretch satan's slaves.</center>
<center>Each washed - not drowned - by that same precious blood.</center>

<center>A story without teeth won't do anything much.</center>
<center>But this story has teeth to keep changing my heart.</center>
