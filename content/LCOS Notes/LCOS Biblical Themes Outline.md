#  **Biblical Themes**  

**Temple (Heaven+Earth)**
+ Yahweh’s presence  
+ Layout of the temple alluded to  
+ Garden of Eden imagery/Mountains  
+ Access by sacrifice  
+ Yahweh’s space and people’s space  
+ Presence of spiritual beings

**Sabbath**
+ Number 7 is used to signal theme  
+ Rest from \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_  
+ Cycles of time

**Image of God**
+ Rulers/caretakers on behalf of God  
+ Priests in the temple

**Tree of Life**
+ Trees/bushes/wood  
+ Choices made to either follow Yahweh or define good and bad for yourself

**Exile**
+ “East of \_\_\_\_\_\_” / “Down to \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_”  
+ “Babylon”/ “Egypt”/ “Assyria”  
+ Wilderness (Number 40\)  
+ Temple destroyed

**Water of Life**
+ Water/wells/rivers/springs/streams  
+ Water brings life out of desolation  
+ Water rescues

**Day of Yahweh**
+ “Day of the Lord”/”The Day”  
+ Passover/Exodus referenced or alluded to
  
**(Covenant)**
+ “God of your fathers, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob”  
+ Agreements made at mountains/trees/springs
