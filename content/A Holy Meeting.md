#🍋Fruit   #✒️Poem  #🔥Doctrine-Poetry-Story 
****

<center>Snap!  The fetters charged to hold firm this world fail – reality is lost in light.</center>
<center>Eyes see yet closed, or do eyes see at all?  Crisp haze covers all.</center>
<center>Love bleeds through the air and whispers, “come.”</center>
<center>What can be said?  No sound but a voice, no sight but a scene,</center><center>My mind conceives but cannot understand.  Love calls again:</center>
<center>“Come my son; come see sights from above.”</center>
<center>What are these sights Lord?  Can words wrap them in wondrous clothes?</center>
<center>Nay, words dress them dank and dull their hue.  Love speaks louder still:</center>
<center>No words… love’s voice is loudest in silence.</center>
<center>The earth speaks at love’s command and bends to its Creator’s hand.</center>
<center>Aspen tree groves stand strait like sleek white pillars in His estate.</center>
<center>I feel (or do I hear) something coming,</center>
<center>But it is not yet here.  The sun smiles on through leafy green bows,</center>
<center>Stars take their turn dispelling darkness, like jewels on Heaven’s crown.</center>
<center>These are but love’s soft voice in earth’s vast frame.</center>
<center>A pulse now grabs the air; it stirs the woods - they quiver, shiver,</center>
<center>The time grows nigh as He approaches – peace shimmers bright,</center>
<center>My little voice cries:  “Holy Spirit, come.”</center>
<center>Aspen branches bend and bow the winds swirl thither – now hither.</center>
<center>Earthly laws mean nothing now; I stand in awe, my heart amazed:</center>
<center>“Jesus, true Jesus, I ask you to come.”</center>
<center>Heaven’s warm breath swells, sways, love gushes over this little place,</center>
<center>Eternity breaths into time and space – peaceful power flows.</center>
<center>“Come, Father God, come in this place to dwell.”</center>
<center>Veins fill with blood, yet more now tingles thorough this mortal frame:</center>
<center>Seeing the unreachable, feeling the imperceivable.</center>
<center>Alas it melts and earth reclaims its captive,</center>
<center>This minute spent in my Master’s arms moves into mental stores.</center>
<center>Call it a vision, call it a dream, the time will come when it is reality.</center>
