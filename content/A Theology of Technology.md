Technology is not value neutral. Every piece of technology works to realize a particular picture of human thriving and existence. In other words, it take on a particular kind of work in order to (theoretically) make the lives of the humans using it better. A good example of this is the automobile. This piece of technology had vastly changed the ability of humans to personally inhabit a much larger area than would be possible on foot, or with other more ridged forms of transportation. 

We come from a long history of Christians who have harness new technology for the good and spread of the Gospel. Martin Luther famously put the brand new printing press to great use during the reformation. Paul made extensive use of the then state of the art road and boat system throughout the Roman empire during his missionary journeys. Yet even with these positive examples every technology has a threshold after which they no longer serve as an aid but become a liability and even an addiction.  Luther made strong use of the printing press for good but Hitler and Stalin both had extensive printing and publicity programs within their regimes.  Paul traveled far and wide on roman roads, but the romans themselves used these same roads to bring crushing taxes and physical oppression to huge populations of people.
