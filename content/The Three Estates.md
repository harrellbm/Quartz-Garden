>[!Summary] The Big Idea
> Thinking of How got sets up authority in the world

****

In each estate there exists a set of hierarchically structured relationships that organize human life under God’s care. The top of each hierarchy stands God himself who endows those ruling and governing in the given hierarchy with their given authority. The basic premise of all hierarchies is that the authority that subsists in each is finally divine.

Thus the state consists of rulers and the ruled. The household, at its most basic level consists of parents and children. And the church consists of clergy and laity.
