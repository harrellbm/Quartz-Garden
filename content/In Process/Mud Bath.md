Culture comes from the Latin colere which means to "to tend, guard; to till, cultivate"[^1]
Culture has often turned into something that needs defended. Or that is fought over. But when we think of the original metaphor of "preparing the soil." Culture becomes a lot clearer to identify. It is all the stuff that makes up the soil within which we grow our lives.
