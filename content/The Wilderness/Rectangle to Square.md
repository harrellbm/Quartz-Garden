> [!Summary] The Big Idea
> Recently I have realized that all the patterns for things that are built given by God have a particular shape to them that heads in a set direction.

---

Exod 25:9 - Tabernacle by the pattern God shows. It's major outlines are all rectangles except the holy of holies that is square.

Solomon's temple as well as the second temple all follow the general rectangle with a square holy of holies

Ark is also built by a design given by God
