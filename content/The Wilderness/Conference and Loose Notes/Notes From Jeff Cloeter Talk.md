# “Muscles to Flex”

Muscles to Flex – I’ll name Seven of them, a good Biblical number.

## Pioneering Muscle

You are on the frontier. I am in St. Louis. What you are experiencing now, we will in the years to come. I’m a learner. I love to learn from you. I’ve been intentionally doing that for over ten years.

## Relational Muscle

“Earn the right to be heard” (a Young Life phrase). Relational Capital. How are we earning the right to be heard? – Acts 2:47 – 1 Timothy 3:2,7 – In our day the challenge is to move from “positional authority” to “relational authority”

## Collaborative Muscle

Selfless – mutual – multi-congregational. No room to compare and compete. What is first step to collaborate? – John 17 – We are each other. We are one another.

## Apprenticeship Muscle

Joshua (with Moses). Timothy (with Paul). Whether Pastor, Elder, Christian businessperson, etc. Always asking: “Who’s next?” Actively looking and mentoring. – Event in St. Louis, September 17-19 “Loved & Sent” – will be a “collaborative sent” [lovedsent.org](http://lovedsent.org/)

## Naming Muscle

Honest Reality and Hopeful Response. What is it? Can you imagine? … “Weaponized Nostalgia” … What are we doing wrong that we can’t regain the past? Have we messed up? Not like it used to be. Inadequate. Please remember: Jesus is still the Senior Pastor of the Church. He’s perfectly suited.

## Sacrificial Muscle

Do you know John Daly, the golfer? He doesn’t look like a golfer. He once said … “You can pull a muscle. So, I’m fat—you can’t pull fat.” He loves McDonalds.  Sometimes we get flabby. Lazy. Spoiled. We depend more on our own resources and less on the Spirit. Would you crawl across broken glass for the Gospel?

## Pentecostal Muscle

Now don’t get all excited about what I call this next muscle: “Pentecostal” – not like you might think of that word. Rather, Pentecost – as in day of. PRAYER _BEFORE_ Planning. SPIRIT _OVER_ Strategy. Story about meeting with a foreign-born people-group in St. Louis talking planning and strategy. We batted things around for quite some time. Finally, someone said, “Pastor, do you think maybe we should pray about this?” (What a novel idea!) “Yes, I think we should.” I closed my eyes and folded my hands and began a prayer … then I paused … I opened my eyes a bit, and each of them were literally down on their knees. I got down on mine, too. We were there on our knees on the floor for thirty-five minutes. I learned something that day. PRAYER _BEFORE_ Planning. SPIRIT _OVER_ Strategy. Psalm 85 – “Revive us again, O Lord!” Great is our challenge. Greater is our Savior.

## Conclusion

We are the Bridge, the Hinge between generations. Ask people questions: What do you think might reach your kids? Your grandkids? Name reality. You may not like young people today (their dress, their devices, …). Think: What questions did I have as a youth? Not seeing young people in a certain setting, we asked them in another, “Why not plugged in?” Research has shown that kids tend to stick with a church if five adults (other than their parents) know their name. You succeed in the Army by joining forces.
