> [!Summary] The Big Idea
> Coram Deo translated literally means "before God" and refers to our relationship with God. It is also pictured as the vertical up and down relationship we have with God. This relationship is completely broken by human sin and so we receive only Passive (or Alien) righteousness from God on account of the death and resurrection of Jesus. This relationship is all about us humbly receiving the work of God in our lives to restore our relationship with Him apart from anything we could ever do.
