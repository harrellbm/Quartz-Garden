>[!quote] St. Francis DeSales, Bishop of Geneva 1602-1622, Introduction to the Devout Life
> **Strive to See God in all things without exception**, and acquiesce in His well with absolute submission. Do everything for God, uniting yourself to Him by a mere upward glance, or by the overflowing of your heart towards Him. **Never be in a hurry**; do everything **quietly and in a calm spirit.** Do not lose your inward peace for anything whatsoever, even if your world seems upset. Commend all to God, and then **lie still and be at rest in His bosom**. Whatever happens, abide steadfast in a determination to cling simply to God, trusting in His eternal love for you; and if (when) you find that you have wandered forth from this shelter, **recall your heart quietly and simply**. Maintain a holy simplicity of mind, and do not smother yourself with a host of cares, wishes, or longings, under any pretext. 
>
